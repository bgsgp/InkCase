The Value of a Pause

议论文　·　约 320 词　·　中等难度

In a world that celebrates speed, choosing to pause can feel like falling behind. Yet a moment of stillness often gives us the clarity that constant motion cannot.

When we stop reacting and start observing, we notice what truly matters. The pause is not empty time; it is where better decisions begin.

This is why great teachers leave room for questions. Silence invites students to arrange their thoughts, rather than simply repeat an answer.
